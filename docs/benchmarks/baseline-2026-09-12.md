**Turn-row measurement boundary.** `turn/scripted/tools_4/builtin` is an **in-process inner-turn** measurement. It is **not** comparable to the child-process rows. Only `turn/scripted/tools_4/builtin_child` shares the child-process boundary with `hashline_ext` and `inactive_ext_8`. Subtracting a child-boundary p50 from the in-process `builtin` p50 is invalid.

No extension-overhead figure is claimed. At n=100 `hashline_ext` is ~15 ms p50 slower than `builtin_child` (42.16 ms vs 27.48 ms, against a p95-p50 spread of ~5 ms). The gap is unattributed: it may be extension-host round-trip, activation, or tool-path differences. Attribution needs `extension_id` on the tool marks.

On this class of machine a large fraction of scripted-turn wall time is session-log fsync. These numbers reflect **one durable sync inside the turn, plus one at session start**. `turn/phase/session_persisted` and `turn/phase/turn_completed` are child-process wall times through the scripted-turn sampler and include that durability; they are not a separate fsync-cost claim.

The in-process headless `#alloc_*` rows exclude one-time process-global render initialisation. That is the #271 harness change, not a product allocation win: `startup/backend_ready_to_first_interactive_headless#alloc_count` is 102 per sample (10200 across n=100), stable. Do not compare those rows to a pre-#271 snapshot.
